=======================================================================
PURPOSE
=======================================================================

This SOP defines the standard workflows for Patient Support Program
(PSP) operations, including patient identification, enrollment,
engagement, and adherence monitoring.

=======================================================================
SECTION 1: PATIENT IDENTIFICATION AND RISK STRATIFICATION
=======================================================================

1.1 ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Patients are eligible for active support program enrollment when they
meet one or more of the following criteria:
  - Diagnosed with a chronic condition on a qualifying medication
  - Newly prescribed a qualifying medication (first 90 days)
  - Identified as high-risk by the adherence analytics platform
  - Self-referred or HCP-referred

1.2 RISK STRATIFICATION

All enrolled patients are stratified using the adherence risk model:

  HIGH RISK (Risk Score >= 0.65):
  - Immediate outreach within 48 hours
  - Assigned dedicated case manager
  - Monthly scheduled contact

  MEDIUM RISK (Risk Score 0.40–0.64):
  - Outreach within 5 business days
  - Monthly automated reminders
  - Quarterly HCP notification

  LOW RISK (Risk Score < 0.40):
  - Standard 90-day refill reminder schedule
  - Annual adherence review

1.3 DATA SOURCES FOR RISK IDENTIFICATION

The PSP platform integrates the following data sources:
  - Pharmacy claims (refill dates, gaps, quantities)
  - HCP engagement records
  - Patient demographics (age, insurance, region)
  - Prior adherence history (missed refill count, trends)

=======================================================================
SECTION 2: PATIENT ENROLLMENT WORKFLOW
=======================================================================

STEP 1: ELIGIBILITY VERIFICATION
  - Confirm patient diagnosis and medication eligibility
  - Verify insurance coverage and copay requirements
  - Confirm patient consent for program participation

STEP 2: ONBOARDING
  - Schedule welcome call within 72 hours of enrollment
  - Provide patient education materials on medication adherence
  - Establish preferred contact method (phone, email, SMS, portal)
  - Set refill reminder preferences

STEP 3: PLATFORM ENROLLMENT
  - Enter patient into the adherence monitoring system
  - Assign risk level based on initial assessment
  - Schedule first engagement based on risk level

=======================================================================
SECTION 3: ENGAGEMENT PROTOCOLS
=======================================================================

3.1 REFILL REMINDER PROTOCOL

  HIGH RISK PATIENTS:
  - Day -7: SMS + Phone call reminder before expected refill
  - Day 0:  Email confirmation + pharmacy routing
  - Day +3: Follow-up call if no refill recorded
  - Day +7: Case manager escalation if still no refill
  - Day +14: HCP notification if refill not completed

  MEDIUM RISK PATIENTS:
  - Day -5: SMS reminder
  - Day 0:  Email reminder
  - Day +7: Follow-up if refill not recorded

  LOW RISK PATIENTS:
  - Day -3: SMS reminder only

3.2 MISSED REFILL RESPONSE PROTOCOL

When a missed refill event is detected (actual date > expected + 14 days):

LEVEL 1 RESPONSE (First missed refill):
  - Patient contact attempt within 2 business days
  - Barrier assessment (cost, access, tolerability)
  - Support offered: copay assistance, pharmacy routing, education

LEVEL 2 RESPONSE (Second missed refill within 90 days):
  - Escalate to case manager
  - HCP notification of adherence concern
  - Comprehensive barrier assessment
  - Consider 90-day supply dispensing

LEVEL 3 RESPONSE (Third+ missed refill or 60+ day gap):
  - Flag as chronically non-adherent
  - Urgent HCP notification
  - Consider patient re-education or regimen review
  - Document in patient record

=======================================================================
SECTION 4: REPORTING AND ESCALATION
=======================================================================

4.1 DAILY MONITORING

  Program managers review daily dashboards for:
  - New high-risk patients identified
  - Missed refill events flagged overnight
  - Overdue engagement contacts
  - Pharmacy performance alerts

4.2 WEEKLY REPORTING

  Weekly summary reports include:
  - Total active patients by risk tier
  - Engagement completion rate
  - Refill rate for the week
  - Regional adherence performance
  - New enrollments vs. dropouts

4.3 MONTHLY REPORTING

  Monthly reports include:
  - Population adherence trends (MPR, PDC)
  - Program effectiveness vs. baseline
  - High-risk patient status review
  - Regional and pharmacy performance analysis
  - HCP engagement summary

=======================================================================
SECTION 5: ESCALATION MATRIX
=======================================================================

ESCALATION LEVEL 1 (Case Manager):
  Trigger: Patient has not responded to 2+ contact attempts
  Action: Direct outreach, barrier resolution support

ESCALATION LEVEL 2 (Medical Affairs / HCP):
  Trigger: 60+ day refill gap, patient unresponsive
  Action: HCP notification letter/portal message

ESCALATION LEVEL 3 (Program Director):
  Trigger: Patient requests opt-out or program failure
  Action: Exit interview, document root cause

=======================================================================
SECTION 6: QUALITY ASSURANCE
=======================================================================

6.1 Data Quality:
  - All source data validated within 24 hours of receipt
  - Rejected records reviewed weekly
  - Data quality KPIs tracked monthly

6.2 Engagement Quality:
  - Random sample call audits (10% of contacts monthly)
  - Patient satisfaction surveys quarterly
  - Engagement response rate benchmarks reviewed monthly
