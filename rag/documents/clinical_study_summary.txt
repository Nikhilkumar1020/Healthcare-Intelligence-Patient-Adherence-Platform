SECTION 3: INTERVENTION EFFECTIVENESS FINDINGS
=======================================================================

ENGAGEMENT PROGRAM PARTICIPATION:
Patients who received 3+ engagement contacts in a 90-day period
showed significantly higher refill rates:

  Engaged patients (3+ contacts/90 days):
    - Average adherence: 84.3%
    - Average refill gap: 27.8 days
    - Missed refill rate: 15.7%

  Non-engaged patients (<3 contacts/90 days):
    - Average adherence: 68.9%
    - Average refill gap: 38.1 days
    - Missed refill rate: 31.1%

  Adherence improvement attributable to engagement: +15.4 percentage points

RESPONSE TYPE ANALYSIS:
  - Patients who responded to SMS reminders showed 12% better adherence
  - Phone call responders showed 18% better adherence
  - Portal access correlated with 9% better adherence
  - Non-responders had 2.3× higher missed refill rate

PHARMACY-LEVEL EFFECTS:
  - Top 10 performing pharmacies: average 87.4% adherence
  - Bottom 10 performing pharmacies: average 61.2% adherence
  - Pharmacy performance gap: 26.2 percentage points
  - High-performing pharmacies served 15% more patients per pharmacy

SECTION 5: BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
=======================================================================

Based on the analytical findings, the following business-oriented
prioritization actions are recommended:

1. PRIORITIZE HIGH-RISK PATIENT OUTREACH:
   Focus patient support resources on the 22.4% of patients
   classified as HIGH risk, who represent the greatest opportunity
   for adherence improvement.

2. INVESTIGATE NORTH REGION PERFORMANCE:
   The North region's 72.1% adherence rate (vs. 81.2% in East)
   warrants targeted investigation of pharmacy access, payer mix,
   and engagement program coverage gaps.

3. ADDRESS LOW-PERFORMING PHARMACIES:
   The 26.2 percentage point adherence gap between top and bottom
   pharmacies suggests systemic differences in dispensing practices,
   patient counseling, and refill reminder programs.

4. EXPAND ENGAGEMENT PROGRAMS:
   The 15.4 percentage point adherence improvement associated with
   active engagement (3+ contacts per 90 days) demonstrates clear
   ROI for investing in outreach program capacity.

5. DEPLOY PREDICTIVE RISK SCORING:
   Integrating the ML risk model into daily operations enables
   proactive identification of at-risk patients before they miss a
   refill, rather than reactive response after the fact.
